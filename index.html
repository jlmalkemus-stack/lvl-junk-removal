FAQ

Got questions?

How much does junk removal cost?

It depends on how much you have and what it is. Text a photo to (657) 221-8442 and we'll give you a free, upfront quote — usually within minutes. No hidden fees.

Do I need to move anything to the curb?

Nope. We come to wherever the junk is — garage, backyard, upstairs bedroom — and do all the lifting and loading.

How fast can you come out?

We often have availability the same week, including weekends. Text or call to check our next opening.

What happens to my stuff?

Usable items get donated whenever possible, recyclables get recycled, and the rest is disposed of properly at licensed facilities.

Is there anything you can't take?

We can't haul hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos or medical waste. If you're unsure about an item, just ask.

Are you really run by high school students?

Yes! LVL Junk Removal is owned and operated by Orange County high school students. Same hard work and care as the big guys — with a lot more hustle.
